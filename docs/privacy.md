# Legacy Dynasty privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Legacy Dynasty is a companion app and website for a private salary-cap fantasy football league. Only league owners invited by the commissioner can sign in.

## What we collect

- **Your account**: the email address and password you sign in with. Passwords are stored by our sign-in provider (Supabase) and are never visible to us.
- **League data**: your team, contracts, cap, lineups and transactions. Most of it comes from the league's public Sleeper data.
- **What you do in the app**: players you save as targets, keep-or-walk decisions, IR moves, and your Front Office chats.

## How we use it

We use it only to run the league and the app: showing your team, applying league rules, and answering your Front Office questions. Front Office questions and the league data needed to answer them are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write the answer. Anthropic does not use that data to train its models.

## What we don't do

- No ads, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
- We never sell or share your data with anyone outside the league and the services above (Supabase for accounts and data, Google Cloud for our server, Anthropic for Front Office answers).

## Your choices

You can sign out at any time in Settings. To delete your account and data, ask the league commissioner. Your account and your app data (saved targets and Front Office chats) will be removed within 30 days. League records such as past contracts and trades stay in the league history.

## Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
