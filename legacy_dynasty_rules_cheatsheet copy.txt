Legacy Dynasty — V1 Roster/Cap Rules (for app backend)

1) Roster size cap:
   - Max 22 total players per team (Active + IR + Taxi combined).

2) Taxi Squad:
   - 1 player per team per year.
   - Eligibility: rookies only.
   - Cap accounting: counts as two-thirds (2/3) of contract salary while on Taxi.
   - Contract-year accounting: if the rookie is placed on Taxi *before the season starts*, that season does NOT decrement contract years.
   - If activated mid-season, contract year decrements from that season onward.

3) Injured Reserve (IR):
   - Max 1 player on IR per team.
   - Cap accounting: counts as half (1/2) of total salary while on IR.
   - When returning from IR, reverts to full cap accounting pro‑rata.

4) Standings scoring (league result points, not fantasy scoring):
   - 2 points for a win.
   - +1 point for beating the league median for the week.
   - Tiebreaker: season points for the playoffs if the the points are tied

Implementation notes:
- App will enforce roster-size limits at transaction time (add/activate/move).
- Cap validations run on every trade/roster change with role-based overrides for commissioner.
- Contract-year decrement rules evaluated on weekly tick (post-week processing) and on Taxi/IR status changes.

CSV import notes:
- 'owner_team_name' must match exactly one of the names in legacy_dynasty_teams.csv.
- 'pos' example values: QB, RB, WR, TE, K, DEF (use your league's set).
- 'status' must be one of: Active, IR, Taxi.
- 'rookie' must be 'yes' or 'no' (used for Taxi eligibility check).
- 'sleeper_id' is optional but recommended for accurate stat joins later.
