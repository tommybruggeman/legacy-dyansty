Legacy Dynasty is the companion app for salary-cap dynasty fantasy football leagues run on Legacy and Sleeper.

Every owner gets their own front office:
- My Team: your roster, contracts, cap space by season, draft picks and recent moves.
- League: live matchups with projected scores, standings, and every team's roster and cap.
- Market: free agents ranked by points, expiring contracts, and the players you've saved as targets.
- Player pages: weekly points, NFL stats, injury outlook, the next game's matchup and Vegas yardage lines.
- Front Office AI: ask your GM who to start, which trades make sense and how to plan your cap, using your league's own rules.

League data syncs from Sleeper automatically. Sign in with the account your league commissioner set up for you.
