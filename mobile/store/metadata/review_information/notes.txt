Legacy Dynasty is the companion app for a fantasy football league that uses salary caps and multi-year player contracts. The league's commissioner invites each owner, so the app has no public sign-up. The demo account is linked to a real team, so every screen has live data. Tabs: Home, My Team, Front Office (AI assistant), League and Market. Odds shown on player pages are information only; the app has no betting or real-money features. Trades happen in the Sleeper app, which the Trade buttons open.
