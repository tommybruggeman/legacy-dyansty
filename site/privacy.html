Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Legacy Dynasty is a companion app and website for a private salary-cap fantasy football league. Only league owners invited by the commissioner can sign in.

What we collect

  • Your account: the email address and password you sign in with. Passwords are stored by our sign-in provider (Supabase) and are never visible to us.
  • League data: your team, contracts, cap, lineups, bids and transactions. Some of it comes from the league's public data on Sleeper.
  • What you do in the app: players you save as targets, your settings, Front Office questions and answers, and answers you report.
  • Usage: which screens you open and for how long, so we can see which parts of the app are useful. This is stored in our own database, tied to your account, and never shared or used for ads.
  • Crash reports: when the app hits an error, a short technical report (the error, the screen, the app version and iOS version) goes to our server's log. It doesn't include your email or name.
  • Your device's alert token, if you turn on alerts, so league alerts reach your phone.

How we use it

Only to run the league and the app: showing your team, applying league rules, sending the alerts you chose, answering your Front Office questions, and fixing and improving the app. Front Office questions and the league data needed to answer them are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write the answer. Anthropic does not use that data to train its models.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We never sell your data or share it with anyone outside the league, except the services that run the app: Supabase (accounts and data), Google Cloud (our server), Anthropic (Front Office answers), Apple (alerts) and Expo (app updates).

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, any time: in the app under Settings › Account › Delete account, or on the website under Account. This removes your sign-in, Front Office chats, saved targets, settings, alert tokens, usage records and your link to your team right away. League records such as past contracts, trades and lineups stay in the league history so other owners' records still add up. The commissioner has to hand the role to another owner before deleting their own account.

Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 13.
