Help and support

Questions, bugs, or account help for the Legacy Dynasty app and website.

Contact us

Email SUPPORT_EMAIL. We usually answer within two days.

How do I get an account?

Your league commissioner sends you an invite email. Open its link, make a password, and your team is linked to your account. Then sign in to the app with the same email and password.

I signed in but I'm "not on a team yet"

Your login isn't linked to a team. Open the invite link from your commissioner while signed in with the same email, or ask the commissioner to send a new invite.

I forgot my password

Use "Forgot password" on the league website. The app and website share one account.

The Front Office GM gave a bad answer

Tap ⚑ Report under the answer and pick what's wrong. Reports go into our weekly review of the GM's accuracy.

Alerts

Turn alerts on from the card on Home, or in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Legacy Dynasty. Pick which alerts you get in the app's Settings. Alerts never arrive between 11 PM and 8 AM your time.

Delete my account

In the app: Settings › Account › Delete account. On the website: Account › Delete account. Your sign-in and personal data are removed right away. League records like past contracts and trades stay in the league history. See the privacy policy.
